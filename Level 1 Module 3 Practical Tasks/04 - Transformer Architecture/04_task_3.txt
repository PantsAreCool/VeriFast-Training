# Task 3: Compare BERT vs GPT Architectures
Research the specific architectural differences between BERT-base (encoder-only) and GPT-2 small (decoder-only).
Create a comparison table covering: number of layers, hidden dimension, number of attention heads, total parameters, 
training objective (masked language modeling vs next token prediction), attention type (bidirectional vs causal), and the attention mask used. 
Write a paragraph explaining which architecture is better suited for sentiment analysis and which for story generation, and why.

BERT-base (Encoder-Only):
  - Number of Layers: 12
  - Hidden Dimension (d_model): 768
  - Number of Attention Heads: 12
  - Total Parameters: 110 Million
  - Training Objective: Masked Language Modeling (Fill-in-the-blank)
  - Attention Type: Bidirectional (Looks left and right at the same time)
  - Attention Mask Used: Padding Mask (Only hides empty padding spaces)

* GPT-2 small (Decoder-Only):
  - Number of Layers: 12
  - Hidden Dimension (d_model): 768
  - Number of Attention Heads: 12
  - Total Parameters: 117 Million
  - Training Objective: Next Token Prediction (Predicts the very next word)
  - Attention Type: Causal (Looks only at past words)
  - Attention Mask Used: Causal Triangular Mask (Hides all future words)

BERT is much better suited for sentiment analysis. When evaluating whether a review is positive or negative, 
the model needs to understand the entire context of a sentence at once. 
Since BERT uses bidirectional attention, it can read a word while looking at both the words before it and after it simultaneously.

GPT-2 is much better suited for story generation. Writing a story requires predicting one word at a time in a natural, forward-flowing sequence.
GPT-2 uses causal attention, it is strictly trained to look only at past words to guess what comes next.
This prevents the model from accidentally "peeking ahead" into the future during training, making it effective at generating paragraphs of text 
from a creative prompt.
